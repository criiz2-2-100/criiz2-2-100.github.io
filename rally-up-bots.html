Rally Up Bots (HUDDLE)

A local-first AI crew console for your own machine. Six Robos — little office robots wearing shirts printed with 2, 1 and 0 — sit around a roundtable, answer you, challenge each other, use tools and show expressions while they think. Runs on Ollama: offline, free.
